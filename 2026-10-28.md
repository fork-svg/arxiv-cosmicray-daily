## 1. CROMA: a GPU-accelerated Fokker--Planck solver for modeling non-thermal emission in post-processing cosmological simulations

- **arXiv**: [2610.36074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36074)
- **作者**: Kosuke Nishiwaki, Claudio Gheller, Gianfranco Brunetti, Katsuaki Asano

**摘要**: Numerical modeling of cosmic-ray (CR) spectra and the resulting non-thermal emission is important for understanding the physical mechanisms of CR acceleration and transport in astrophysical environments. Galaxy clusters and cosmic filaments provide particularly important environments for studying these processes, where shocks, turbulence, magnetic fields, and CRs jointly shape diffuse synchrotron emission. Spectrally resolved modeling of CRs, including cooling, re-acceleration, and hadronic interactions, requires solving time-dependent Fokker--Planck (FP) equations. An increasing number of studies employ FP solvers coupled with magnetohydrodynamical (MHD) simulations to follow the evolution of CRs in dynamically evolving environments. We present a new C/C++-based FP solver with GPU acceleration for post-processing outputs from cosmological MHD simulations. The code evolves CR proton and electron spectra along Lagrangian tracer trajectories and consistently includes re-acceleration and secondary-particle production processes. With the Chang--Cooper finite-difference scheme, the FP equation for each tracer is reduced to an independent tridiagonal system, which is solved on NVIDIA GPUs using a CUDA implementation of the parallel cyclic reduction (PCR) algorithm. The code uses sub-stepping between tracer snapshots, with the timestep constrained by the relevant CR cooling or re-acceleration timescale, and a batching algorithm that groups tracers with similar sub-stepping patterns for efficient execution. We find that the GPU acceleration increases the per-node throughput by a factor of $\approx$10 on the Leonardo Booster system operated by CINECA. The fast FP solver is useful for high-resolution simulations dedicated to comparison with next-generation radio observations and future multi-messenger applications combining radio, gamma-ray, and high-energy neutrino observations.

## 2. Reconstruction of very inclined air showers with radio data and application to the first GRANDProto300 cosmic-ray candidates

- **arXiv**: [2610.36282](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36282)
- **作者**: Marion Guelfand, Valentin Decoene, Olivier Martineau, Simon Prunet, Sebastian Castro-Isern, Nathan Lebas

**摘要**: Radio detection of very inclined extensive air showers (EAS) offers a promising way to study ultra-high-energy cosmic rays (UHECRs) and neutrinos above $10^{17}$ eV. We present a method to reconstruct the arrival direction and energy of EAS with zenith angles above $60^\circ$, applicable in principle to upward-going neutrino-induced showers. The approach combines a point-source-like description of the radio wavefront with the Angular Distribution Function (ADF), a phenomenological model describing the angular pattern of electric field amplitudes in the 50-200 MHz band. On realistic cosmic-ray simulations, we achieve better than $0.07^\circ$ resolution on arrival direction and ~15\% on primary energy. Applied directly to voltage traces, this method allows efficient cosmic-ray selection with accurate direction reconstruction and first-order energy estimation. We illustrate the method on the first cosmic-ray candidates detected by the GRANDProto300 prototype of GRAND, validating its detection principle and paving the way for high-precision neutrino searches.

## 3. 2:1 Harmonics in the Radio Light Curves of Supermassive Black Hole Binary Candidates

- **arXiv**: [2610.36732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36732)
- **作者**: A. C. S. Readhead, M. F. Aller, R. D. Blandford, T. Surti, P. V. De la Parra, A. G. Sullivan, P. Mróz, N. Murray, M. C. Begelman, B. Molina, M. L. Lister, E. R. Most, S. O&#39;Neill, V. A. Diamantopou

**摘要**: We present evidence of 2:1 harmonic periodicities in two supermassive black hole binary (SMBHB) candidates (PKS J0805-0111 and PKS 2131-021) in the Owens Valley Radio Observatory (OVRO) + University of Michigan Radio Astronomical Observatory (UMRAO) monitoring programs. This potential discovery follows the previous detection of a 2:1 harmonic periodicity in the SMBHB candidate J1309+1154 from these programs. If confirmed, these suggest a physical origin of blazars exhibiting strong periodicities favoring 2:1 commensurabilities in their light curves. There are significant differences between the 2:1 harmonics in these three blazars, which may be due to differences in their central engines. These three SMBHB candidates demonstrate the importance of careful dissection, i.e. cutting apart and close examination, of blazar light curves, to reveal underlying evidence of coherence between different aspects of the periodicities observed in blazars in the presence of correlated random noise variations. This is particularly important for blazar SMBHB searches, given that the observed periods thus far are all longer than 2 years, and can disappear for long timespans. Therefore confirming evidence, such as harmonics, may be crucial in confirming the reality of periodicities, and identifying blazar SMBHB candidates. We present four models that can account for 2:1 commensurable periodicities in blazar light curves, together with explanations for why, on these models, shorter period SMBHB candidates have not thus far been observed, but might be anticipated, or not, in the future, depending on the model, thereby making these models discriminatory. Arguments pro and con the reality of these periodicities are presented.

## 4. A microscopic analysis of shear acceleration in the relativistic regime

- **arXiv**: [2610.36867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36867)
- **作者**: Sophie Aerdker, Xingwei Gong, Martin Lemoine, Frank Rieger

**摘要**: Relativistic shear flows, as encountered in jets of powerful astrophysical sources, offer promising sites of particle acceleration up to the highest energies. This stochastic acceleration process is usually described with Fokker-Planck transport equations, which assume small momentum gains per scattering. The present paper introduces a covariant microscopic description of relativistic shear acceleration, building on the formalism of generalized Fermi acceleration and formulating angular scattering in the locally comoving frame in terms of stochastic differential equations. This allows us to model relativistic shear profiles and to study the transition from gradual to non-gradual shear acceleration, where the particle mean free path becomes comparable to the shear length scale. Assuming an infinite shear layer, linear in the flow four-velocity, we observe that the shear rate peaks where the flow is mildly relativistic, whereas Lorentz aberration suppresses the transverse velocity of particles where the flow is relativistic. Consequently, particles that leave the central layer experience long waiting times with a heavy-tailed, power-law distribution. The momentum evolution becomes subdiffusive and the acceleration time can substantially exceed local estimates. We successfully reproduce the Monte Carlo energy distributions using a two-state continuous-time random walk with Lévy-distributed waiting times. We also show how a momentum-dependent mean-free path can introduce breaks in the spectrum and extend our discussion to finite jet profiles. These effects should be accounted for in models of relativistic jets as sources of high-energy particles.

## 5. Discovery of a New Electron Spectrum Produced by Fast Cooling of Relativistic Electrons

- **arXiv**: [2610.37044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37044)
- **作者**: Z. Lucas Uhm

**摘要**: In energetic astrophysical sources like the gamma-ray bursts (GRBs) and blazars, the relativistic electrons lose their energy efficiently through synchrotron radiation and inverse Compton (IC) scattering and can rapidly cool to energies below the minimum acceleration energy $\gamma_m$. It is widely known that such electrons develop a power-law spectrum $\gamma_e^{-2}$ below $\gamma_m$. However, this power-law spectrum is valid only if a steady-state population of electrons is assumed. In general, the cooled electrons below $\gamma_m$ evolve in a non-steady state and their spectrum deviates significantly from the power-law $\gamma_e^{-2}$. We present here a new electron spectrum $f_{\rm U}$ that describes this fast-cooled, generally-evolving, and non-steady-state population of electrons below $\gamma_m$. We consider two cooling cases: (1) when the electrons undergo radiative (i.e., synchrotron + IC) cooling, and (2) when the electrons experience both radiative and adiabatic cooling. We first derive $f_{\rm U}$ analytically and find an explicit expression of $f_{\rm U}$ for each cooling case. Secondly, we show that $f_{\rm U}$ is a solution to a Fokker-Planck equation in the entire energy range below $\gamma_m$ and derive a general version of $f_{\rm U}$ for each cooling case. We also compare $f_{\rm U}$ to a detailed numerical calculation of a blazar electron spectrum and find an excellent agreement for the whole energy range below $\gamma_m$. Thus, $f_{\rm U}$ gives us a general electron spectrum to describe the entire cooled electrons below $\gamma_m$. Our discovery of a new fast-cooling solution $f_{\rm U}$ below $\gamma_m$ may find it applicable in a wide range of energetic phenomena in high-energy astrophysics.

## 7. Coherent Radio Emission from Flux Rope Mergers in 3D Relativistic Reconnection

- **arXiv**: [2610.37214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37214)
- **作者**: Jasmine Parsons, Lorenzo Sironi, Anatoly Spitkovsky, Alexander Chernoglazov

**摘要**: Coherent emission from merging plasmoids in relativistic reconnection is the leading model for radio emission originating in the outer magnetosphere of pulsars such as the Crab, and a candidate emission mechanism for fast radio bursts. This process has only been studied in 2D, and whether it survives in 3D, where the dynamics of structures in the reconnection layer can differ substantially, is still an open question. Here we present the first study of coherent wave generation from flux rope mergers in 3D relativistic reconnection. We use particle-in-cell simulations of relativistic reconnection with outflow boundary conditions and guide fields $B_g/B_0 = \{0,0.1,0.3\}$, and measure the efficiency $\eta$ with which inflowing magnetic energy is converted into escaping coherent waves. We find $\eta \sim 7\times10^{-4}$ without a guide field, a factor of a few below 2D results, and $\eta\sim(1\text{-}2) \times10^{-3}$ at moderate guide fields, comparable to 2D with no guide field. We propose a physical picture for why the efficiency remains comparable to 2D, based on how reconnected field is transported away from the diffusion regions. The escaping waves are linearly polarized, with a polarization angle set by the direction of the upstream field. In the presence of a guide field, waves escaping from opposite sides of the reconnection layer therefore differ in polarization angle by $ \sim 2\arctan(B_g/B_0)$. Our results support relativistic magnetic reconnection as the origin of coherent radio emission from Crab-like pulsars, and strengthen its case as a potential emission mechanism for fast radio bursts.

## 10. $γ$-ray variability of changing-look blazars: FSRQ-like jet behaviour

- **arXiv**: [2610.37303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37303)
- **作者**: Zahoor Malik, B. van Soelen, Sikandar Akbar, Zahir Shah

**摘要**: Changing-look blazars (CLBs) move between the flat-spectrum radio quasar (FSRQ) and BL Lacertae (BL Lac) optical classifications. Either the accretion flow switches regime, or a brightening jet continuum swamps the broad lines while the system itself is unchanged. The two pictures predict the same optical behaviour but differ in the jet, so the $\gamma$-ray emission could potentially separate them. Catalogue quantities averaged over the mission cannot, because a permanently intermediate source and one alternating between two states return the same mean. We therefore use 17.9\,yr of \textit{Fermi}-LAT light curves at 3-, 7- and 30-day cadence, comparing CLBs with BL Lac and FSRQ controls selected and analysed identically. CLBs and FSRQs have indistinguishable fractional variability, with medians of 0.483 and 0.437 at 30-day cadence ($p = 0.94$), while BL Lacs are far less variable at 0.262 ($p = 1.4\times10^{-4}$). The result survives restricting the controls to low-synchrotron-peaked sources and matching in flux and sampling. Several further diagnostics group CLBs with FSRQs, as does their Eddington-scaled disc luminosity. Followed through time, CLB light curves show no excess of bimodal flux distributions, and the direction of the optical transition does not predict how the $\gamma$-ray spectrum evolves. CLBs do reach hard, BL Lac-like spectra more often than FSRQs, but never for longer than chance allows. Only in this, and in how their spectra respond to brightening, do they fall between the two classes. The changing-look phenomenon therefore leaves little imprint on the $\gamma$-ray jet, favouring a variable jet continuum over a change of accretion regime.

## 11. Impact of Neutrino Trapping on the Equation of State and Structure of Hybrid Stars with 2SC Quark Matter

- **arXiv**: [2610.37360](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37360)
- **作者**: Andrea Sabatucci, Armen Sedrakian

**摘要**: We investigate how sensitive the effects of neutrino trapping on hot hybrid stars are to uncertainties in the hadronic and quark equations of state and in the thermodynamic construction of the hadron--quark transition. The hadronic phase is described by finite-temperature covariant density functional equations of state, and the quark phase by a three-flavor Nambu--Jona-Lasinio (NJL) model with repulsive vector interactions and two-flavor color superconductivity. Because confinement is absent from the NJL model, an effective pressure shift $B^*$ is used phenomenologically to locate the deconfinement transition. We construct isentropic stellar equations of state with and without trapped electron neutrinos, vary the quark vector coupling and the nuclear symmetry-energy slope, and compare global with local conservation of electron-lepton number within phase coexistence. Neutrino trapping generically shifts deconfinement toward higher density throughout the parameter range considered. Increasing the vector interaction stiffens quark matter and raises the maximum stellar mass, but only moderately for the couplings studied. We also examine stronger diquark pairing and RG-consistent regularization, instead of sharp momentum cut-off: both affect the pairing gap, phase boundaries, and quark-core composition more strongly than they alter the qualitative trends of the stellar mass--radius sequences. Conversely, trapping reduces the sensitivity to the hadronic symmetry-energy slope because the enhanced proton fraction drives matter toward greater isospin symmetry. Global and local lepton-number prescriptions produce rather similar mass--radius relations but substantially different internal phase structures: global conservation leads to a considerably more extended mixed phase.

## 15. Quasinormal Ringing of a Schwarzschild Black Hole Surrounded by a King Dark Matter Halo: Higher-Order WKB Analysis

- **arXiv**: [2610.37501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37501)
- **作者**: Semra Gurtas Dogan, Hassan Hassanabadi, Abdullah Guvendi

**摘要**: We study scalar and electromagnetic perturbations of a Schwarzschild black hole (BH) surrounded by a dark matter halo described by the King density profile. The lapse function can be expressed as $1-2[M+M_{\rm DM}(r)]/r$, where $M_{\rm DM}(r)$ is the halo mass enclosed within radius $r$; thus, the halo affects the geometry through its enclosed-mass function. We compute the quasinormal frequencies using the sixth-order WKB method and its Padé-improved version. Rewriting the lapse function in terms of the inverse hyperbolic sine avoids the severe numerical cancellations that arise when evaluating its high-order derivatives. For the configurations examined, the fundamental frequencies agree with those extracted from time-domain evolution to within $0.2\%$.The halo lowers both the oscillation frequency and the damping rate. The change of the real part is almost entirely accounted for by the halo mass enclosed by the photon sphere, and the additional decrease of the damping rate follows the ratio of the Lyapunov exponent to the angular frequency of the circular null orbit. Since part of the halo lies inside the horizon in this metric, we also place the halo outside the BH by means of an Einstein cluster. The frequencies then decrease by a similar amount, but the real part follows the Schwarzschild value multiplied by the gravitational redshift factor of the halo. A jump in the density at the inner edge of the halo scatters the waves and changes the damping rate by up to about $9\%$. For a continuous density profile, deviations from the redshift relation remain below about $1.5\%$ for $r_{\rm in}=3M$ and below about $0.5\%$ for the cases with $r_{\rm in}\geq4M$. No unstable modes are found within the perturbation sectors and parameter configurations examined.

## 17. On the Physical Origins of the Millimeter Fundamental Plane in Active Galactic Nuclei

- **arXiv**: [2601.10612](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10612)
- **作者**: Kratika Mazde, Angelo Ricarte, George N. Wong

**摘要**: Observations of active galactic nuclei have revealed a correlation between millimeter luminosity, X-ray luminosity, and mass, suggesting the emission in each of these bands is powered by a common source. ## 20. Flavomons in Matter Gradients: Ray Tracing and Amplitude Evolution

## 21. An Arbitrary-Lagrangian-Eulerian solver for relativistic detonation waves

- **arXiv**: [2606.27113](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27113)
- **作者**: Sara Rinaldi, Olindo Zanotti, Michael Dumbser

**摘要**: In this paper we study the dynamics of relativistic detonation waves theoretically and numerically. The reaction is physically accounted for by an extra term in the definition of the total energy density and by an additional equation for the evolution of the mass fraction of the reactant, while leaving formally unmodified the equations of mass and energy-momentum conservation.
