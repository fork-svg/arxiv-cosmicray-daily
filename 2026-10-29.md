### 6. Discovery of a New Electron Spectrum Produced by Fast Cooling of Relativistic Electrons

**中文标题**: 发现 的 一个新的 Electron 谱 Produced 通过 Fast Cooling 的 Relativistic Electrons
**arXiv**: [2610.37044](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37044)
**作者**: Z. Lucas Uhm
**分类**: γ射线天文 / CR 能谱与传播

**摘要要点**: In energetic astrophysical 源 like the γ射线 bursts (GRBs) and 耀变体, the relativistic electrons lose their 能量 efficiently through 同步辐射 辐射 and 逆康普顿 (IC) 散射 and can rapidly cool to 能量 below the 最小 加速 能量 $\gamma_m$. It is widely known that such electrons develop a power-law 谱 $\gamma_e^{-2}$ below $\gamma_m$.

### 12. Faint and Fast Transients from Double Detonations of Low Mass White Dwarfs

**中文标题**: Faint 与 Fast 暂现源 来自 Double Detonations 的 Low 质量 White Dwarfs
**arXiv**: [2610.37507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37507)
**作者**: Miranda Pikus, Abigail Polin, Dillon Hasenour, Paul Duffell
**分类**: 其他相关

**摘要要点**: There is significant 证据 that white dwarfs can explode via the double detonation 机制, whereby an initial detonation in an accreted helium shell triggers a second detonation of the carbon-oxygen core. This 情景 has been invoked for white dwarfs near a solar 质量 to produce 类型 Ia-like supernovae, but low-质量 explosions have been relatively unexplored despite the most common white dwarf being near 0.6 solar 质量.

### 13. Coherent Radio Emission from Flux Rope Mergers in 3D Relativistic Reconnection

**中文标题**: Coherent 射电 辐射 来自 流量 Rope Mergers 在 3D Relativistic Reconnection
**arXiv**: [2610.37214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37214)
**作者**: Jasmine Parsons, Lorenzo Sironi, Anatoly Spitkovsky, Alexander Chernoglazov
**分类**: 其他相关

**摘要要点**: Coherent 辐射 from merging plasmoids in relativistic reconnection is the leading 模型 for 射电 辐射 originating in the outer magnetosphere of pulsars such as the Crab, and a 候选体 辐射 机制 for fast 射电 bursts. This 过程 has only been studied in 2D, and whether it survives in 3D, where the dynamics of 结构 in the reconnection layer can differ substantially, is still an open question.

### 14. Boosted Dark Matter Directionality in Large Liquid Scintillator Detectors

**中文标题**: 增强暗物质 Directionality 在 Large Liquid Scintillator Detectors
**arXiv**: [2610.31666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31666)
**作者**: Samuel S. H. Tse, Qishan Liu, Kenny C. Y. Ng, Koun Choi, Yu-Feng Li
**分类**: 暗物质与宇宙线 / CR 能谱与传播

**摘要要点**: Light 暗物质 can be boosted by 宇宙线-暗物质 相互作用, which opens the possibility of direct-探测 searches with large 中微子 detectors. We consider the 信号 produced by 增强暗物质 undergoing quasi-elastic 散射 off carbon and knocking out a neutron in a large liquid scintillator detector.
